Our craft

Every pattern tells the maker's hand.

Each textile piece begins with resist-dye techniques passed through generations — wax or thread reserved in patterns before the cloth meets the indigo vat.

We work directly with a small network of independent makers, paying fairly and keeping batch sizes small enough that no two runs are quite identical.
